# Design and reconstruction acceptance gates

## Gate 0 — Evidence
Original source files manifest, provenance and confidence table. Local/private storage verified. Reject unpublished assumptions.

## Gate 1 — Existing-shell fidelity
Three source photographs vs three camera-matched Blender renders in same paired views. Roof, ceiling, beams, posts, windows and openings align within explicitly marked limits. Scan-labelled floor dimensions preserved where axis interpretation is validated. No imaginary partitions, raised roofs, false loft geometry, or spurious survey claims.

## Gate 2 — Actual equipment fidelity
Separate console model and paired monitors verified against original Sunny Suite photography and measurement. Count exactly two Geithain mains. Centreline, facing direction, stereophonic symmetry, ear/listening distance, sightline and maintainable clearances documented. Orientation is a design decision, not visually guessed.

## Gate 3 — Sound/design coordination
Acoustic consultant checklist: boundary conditions, isolation strategy, flutter/reflections, LF treatment, speaker geometry, ceiling treatment, quiet HVAC path and electrical/patching. Identify where professional tests are mandatory; no fictitious sound-isolation or decay metrics.

## Gate 4 — Live room / working farm
Piano/ensemble mode, machinery storage envelope, exterior garage egress, loft support/guard/fire review and mobile acoustic plan, no new permanent subdivisions. Verify machinery clearances and no machinery operation during recording.

## Gate 5 — Professional views
All approved renders generated from the validated model, with same windows/roof/posts/doors/loft and real equipment. Every view labelled APPROVED GEOMETRY vs DESIGN PROPOSAL; render settings and source linked.

## Gate 6 — Design-development package
Coordinated plans and sections, preliminary assemblies, finishes, MEP/audio infrastructure, salvage plan, budget dependencies, survey/RFI register, structural/architect/acoustic review. No permit/construction-certification claim.

## Stop/rollback
When a gate fails, mark the PR as revision required; do not merge its geometry to main. Record repro steps and affected outputs; seek independent review.

Breakglass East v0.5 — Acoustic Integration & Salvage Optimisation

Status: PROPOSED for owner review. An evolution of the approved v0.4 fireside + cinema design, not a redesign. Not for construction: the shell is a phone-LiDAR + photo reconstruction (±0.1–0.2 m); acoustic numbers are screening ranges that an acoustician and on-site measurement must confirm. Repository branch claude/v05-acoustic-integration, commit 15a29f3. v0.4 files in Drive and Git are unchanged.

1. Concept

  • Absorb where the reflections really land. The trace on the measured tray ceiling puts the strongest early reflections (+9.5 to +15 ms) on the sloped and flat ceiling around Y 4.1–4.5 m. Four Clark GOB panels form a ceiling ‘halo’ exactly there.
  • Deep bass control in the low side zones (new under-sill bench traps, windows kept), corner traps, Clark tapered traps in the rear corners.
  • Quiet front wall: Clark Dead Room Roxul panels on 200 mm wool-filled stand-off frames, clear of the stove.
  • Scattering rear wall: the two Clark fin diffusers flank the entry door; a wool curtain covers the door (also film blackout).
  • Fireside lounge corrected: every lounge seat now faces the stove glass; one armchair rolls back for films.
  • Live room stays a barn: Clark barrels, perforated panels, rustic wood wall and gobos make it variable; machinery bays and the aisle to the garage door are untouched.

2. Matched-camera comparisons (v0.4 approved → v0.5 proposed)

Same camera, lens, samples and resolution; only the design differs.

Erratum found in owner review: in the v0.4 model the rear overhead (Ltr/Rtr) immersive speakers pointed 35° up, 80° off the listening position, and the front pair (Ltf/Rtf) were 10° off. v0.5 aims all four at the ears (0.0° off, 45° down), and the build now fails if any immersive speaker is more than 2° off the listening position. Positions were always correct; v0.4 files are left as approved.

How to read this: ranges apply generic material-class bounds to the measured areas of the allocated assets — they are not the Clark panels' data, and the existing wall lining is unknown. They show direction: the recommended control room reaches the 0.36 s target at mid/high frequencies at its lower bound, while below 125 Hz it may stay long — which is why the new deep LF traps are in the base scope and more can be added after measurement.

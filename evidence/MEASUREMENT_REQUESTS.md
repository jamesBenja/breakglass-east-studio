# Measurement and evidence requests — ranked by impact

## Priority 1 — native scan export (replaces most of the list below)
From the iPhone LiDAR app that produced the screenshots in *3d renderings raw*, open the barn scan and export:
1. **Mesh with textures**: OBJ (zip) or GLB/glTF.
2. **Point cloud**: PLY, LAS or XYZ, at the highest density offered.
3. If offered, the app's **floor-plan / DXF** export.

Upload to the same Drive folder. **Do not edit or decimate the files.** Claude will fit planes, re-run the photo solve against the scan, and extend the model to the whole building. If the app or scan is gone, say so and we fall back to a short field survey (Priority 3).

## Priority 2 — actual equipment (Gate 2), at Clark before dismantling
Tape-measure in millimetres and photograph front, side, top and rear (with any model plate):

| Item | Measurements |
|---|---|
| **Spectra Sonics console** | Overall width, depth, height at front edge and at meter bridge; working-surface height; table/stand footprint and leg positions; frame weight if known; number of input/output modules; position of the patch/connector panel |
| **Geithain monitor L and R** (both, separately) | Model name from the rear plate; cabinet W × H × D; driver centre height above stand base; stand height and footprint; current toe-in angle and tweeter height; distance between acoustic centres; distance from each to the listener's ears in the current room |
| **Two timber rack wings** | W × D × H; rack-space count; angle to the console |
| **Patchbays and outboard** | Count of rack units; heat load if known |
| **Clark acoustic panels and wood** | Count; W × H × thickness; core material (mineral wool / foam / unknown); fabric condition; mounting method; any panel datasheets |

## Priority 3 — field checks in the barn (only if Priority 1 is unavailable)
1. Control room: floor to flat ceiling at the centre; floor to underside of beam; beam inset from each side wall (**both sides**, which tests asymmetry item X1); post centres from the gable wall and side walls; post section.
2. All window and door openings: sill, head, jambs measured from the nearest corner.
3. Is there a hatch above the control-room ceiling? Photograph inside it (roof void, rafters, insulation).
4. Exterior: eave height and ridge height (or pitch with a level app), and building outside length and width.
5. Loft: joist size, spacing and span; floor-to-rafter headroom at the centre; access method.
6. Intermediate zone: door positions; floor level change at the tile area.
7. Live room: garage door clear opening W × H; machinery footprint and operating envelope (each machine L × W × H plus turning path).

## Priority 4 — acoustics (low cost, high value), empty building
- Background-noise survey: day and night, overnight if possible, with the HVAC/stove off and on.
- Impulse responses (balloon or sweep) at 3 positions per room → RT60 by octave.
- Owner to arrange a studio acoustician for this, or I can write a DIY protocol (REW + measurement mic) if preferred.
